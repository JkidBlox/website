// ABOUT THE GAME

Radiopair is a simulation game being developed by Dark Plant Entertainment. The player starts their work inside an office, where they use a computer to select and manage different jobs.

After selecting a job, the player is transported to the work location where they must complete various tasks. The game focuses on exploration, interaction, simulation mechanics, and building a unique work environment.

Radiopair is currently in active development and more features, locations, and gameplay systems are planned for the future.
